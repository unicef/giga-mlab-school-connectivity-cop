# Connectivity Community of Practice

Open repository for the Connectivity Community of Practice — a collaboration between M-Lab and Giga to advance rigorous, globally comparable Internet measurement for schools and public facilities.

## About 

The Connectivity Community of Practice (CCoP) was launched in March 2026 by Measurement Lab and Giga to advance connectivity measurement that is technically rigorous, globally comparable, and practical for real-world contexts, including underserved regions, [read our launch blog post](https://www.measurementlab.net/blog/cop-launch/) to learn more about the community.

The CCoP brings together a diverse membership spanning academia, industry, civil society, international NGOs, researchers, engineers, implementers, policymakers, and advocates, all united by a commitment to open data, open tools, and open collaboration.

**How it works:** We organize regular events, share resources such as code, presentations, and documentation, and actively welcome ideas from all members to shape upcoming session agendas. The community is collaborative by design: members are encouraged to participate in research, form working groups, share findings, and provide feedback.
